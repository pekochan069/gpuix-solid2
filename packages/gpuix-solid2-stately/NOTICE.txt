GPUIX Solid 2 Stately
====================

The build derives its state implementations from @solid2-aria/stately 0.0.1
and its shared declarations from @solid2-aria/types-shared 0.0.1, published
from https://github.com/pekochan069/solid2-aria. Source was audited against
revision 4bb0e1f8c918e63c045c28d995d51dd1cf9a83b1, packages/solid2-stately
and packages/@solid2-aria/types-shared.

Adobe React Spectrum derived files retain their original copyright years,
source paths, and Apache-2.0 notices. Original license texts and notices are
preserved in LICENSE, LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt,
LICENSES/react-spectrum-NOTICE.txt, and LICENSES/solid2-aria-NOTICE.txt.

Modifications for GPUIX: explicit date validation locale, explicit native
virtualizer size inputs, controlled number-step draft restoration,
and renderer-neutral native event, target, file,
and validation declarations. The deterministic build records adaptations
in dist/native-audit.json and marks modified files. Other state algorithms
and translation data are reused without changes. Solid's client runtime
remains an external peer dependency.
